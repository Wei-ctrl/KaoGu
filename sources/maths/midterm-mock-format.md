# 僑先部 數學（第二、三類組）期中模擬考 — Question Format

**Source:** 國立臺灣師範大學僑生先修部 秋季班 期中模擬試卷 — 113 學年度第 1 學期 (pages 2–4) and 114 學年度第 1 學期 (pages 1–3)
**Scope:** functions, lines & slope, exact trig values, limits, continuity, asymptotes
**Exam rules (printed on 114 p.1):** 禁止使用計算機 (No Calculator)；禁止自行攜帶計算紙 (No Calculation Papers)

---

## 1. Fixed structure (same in both years — do not change the counts)

Every paper has **15 + 4 + 5 = 24 questions**.

| Section | Name on paper | Questions | Weight | How to answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 一 | 單選題 | 15 → `<1.>`–`<15.>` | 60% | exactly one of (1)–(5) |
| 二 | 複選題 (113) / 多選題 (114) | 4 → `<16.>`–`<19.>` | 20% | every correct option among (1)–(5) |
| 三 | 選填題 | 5 → A–E | 20% | fill numbered answer slots, starting at `<20.>` |

### Formatting conventions

- Question numbers are written `<n.>`. Fill-in items are lettered **A–E**; their answers go into numbered slots that continue after `<19.>` (113 used `<20.>`–`<32.>`, 114 used `<20.>`–`<33.>`).
- Every 單選 / 多選 question has exactly **5 options**, numbered (1)–(5).
- `∄` is used as an option meaning "does not exist".
- Questions are written in English; key terms are glossed in Chinese in parentheses: slope(斜率), continuous(連續), horizontal(水平的), vertical(垂直的), asymptote(漸近線), oblique(傾斜的), natural domain(自然定義域).
- $[\![\cdot]\!]$ is the greatest integer function; when used, the question adds "($[\![\cdot]\!]$ is the greatest integer function.)".
- Many questions ask for the **correct** option and some for the **wrong / incorrect** one; that keyword is underlined on the paper (underlined + bold in 114).
- 選填題 answers must be in **simplest fraction form (最簡單分數)**. Each slot holds one character (a digit or a minus sign), e.g. an answer of $-\frac{13}{4}$ in a $\frac{\boxed{a}\boxed{b}\boxed{c}}{\boxed{d}}$ layout fills $a=-,\ b=1,\ c=3,\ d=4$.
- Section instructions printed on the paper:
  - 一：(請將答案填入答案卡內，注意題號不要劃錯!!)
  - 二：(請將正確答案填入答案卡內，注意題號不要劃錯!!) — 114 wording: (請將答案填入答案卡內，注意題號不要劃錯!!)
  - 三：(各題的答案，均以最簡單分數作答，劃填至答案卡上指定的題號內，否則不予計分) — 114 wording: (各題均以最簡單分數作答，請將答案填入答案卡內，注意題號不要劃錯!!)
